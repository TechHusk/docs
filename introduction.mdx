## How Husk Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [app.usehusk.com](https://app.usehusk.com), fill in your company address, and verify your email. Onboarding takes about 3 minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your bank">
    Link your existing business bank account via secure open banking (PSD2). Husk pulls in your live balances and transaction history to power insights and credit decisions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    Add team members from the dashboard. Each person gets their own card — no more shared credentials or reimbursements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Issue cards instantly">
    Create a virtual card in 20 seconds or order a physical Mastercard. Set per-card limits, vendor controls, and category rules from day one.
  </Step>
</Steps>
