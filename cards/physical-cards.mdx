Physical Husk cards are Mastercard debit and charge cards that work everywhere Mastercard is accepted — at point-of-sale terminals, ATMs, and for in-person expenses around the world. Each team member can hold one physical card, available on every plan including Free. Cards are shipped to the address you provide during the ordering process and typically arrive within 3–5 business days within Europe.

## One physical card per team member

Every active team member on your Husk account is eligible for one physical Mastercard, regardless of which plan you're on. The card is linked to that team member's profile, giving them their own spending limit, transaction history, and notification settings — all visible to admins in the main dashboard.

## Order a physical card

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Cards section">
    Log in to [app.usehusk.com](https://app.usehusk.com) and navigate to **Cards** in the left-hand menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Issue card">
    Select **Issue card** and then choose **Physical card** from the card-type options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a team member">
    Choose the team member this card is for from the **Assign to** dropdown. If the person is not yet on your account, invite them first via **Account → Team** — see [Invite Your Team](/account/invite-team).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the delivery address">
    Enter or verify the shipping address. Cards can be sent to your registered business address or directly to the team member's location. Double-check the address carefully — redelivery takes an additional 3–5 business days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set an optional spending limit">
    Optionally, configure a daily, monthly, or per-transaction limit before the card is dispatched. You can also set or adjust limits any time after the card arrives — see [Spending Controls](/cards/spending-controls).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place the order">
    Click **Order card**. You'll receive an email confirmation and a tracking update once the card is dispatched.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Delivery timeline

| Region              | Estimated delivery |
|---------------------|--------------------|
| Ireland & UK        | 2–3 business days  |
| EU (eurozone)       | 3–5 business days  |
| Other European countries | 4–7 business days |

<Info>
  Delivery times are estimates and may vary during public holidays or periods of high demand. You'll receive a dispatch confirmation email with a tracking reference when your card ships.
</Info>

## Activate a physical card on arrival

When a physical card arrives, it needs to be activated before it can process transactions.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Cards in the dashboard">
    The card will appear in the **Cards** list with a status of **Inactive — awaiting activation**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Activate card">
    Open the card detail and click the **Activate card** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the last four digits">
    Enter the last four digits of the card number printed on the front of the physical card to confirm receipt, then click **Confirm**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Card is ready">
    The card status updates to **Active**. The cardholder can now use it for purchases immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set a PIN

After activation, set a PIN for use at chip-and-PIN terminals and ATMs.

1. From the card detail page, click **Set PIN**.
2. Enter your chosen 4-digit PIN and confirm it.
3. Your PIN is set and ready to use. For security, the PIN is never stored in plain text and cannot be retrieved — only reset.

To change a forgotten PIN, click **Reset PIN** on the card detail page and follow the same process.

## Adjust spending limits

To update the daily, monthly, or per-transaction limit on a physical card:

1. Click the card in the **Cards** list.
2. Select **Edit limits**.
3. Enter the new limit values and click **Save**.

Limit changes take effect immediately. See [Spending Controls](/cards/spending-controls) for a full description of all available control options.

## Freeze a lost or stolen card

If a physical card is lost or suspected stolen, freeze it immediately to block all new transactions.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the card in the dashboard">
    Navigate to **Cards** and click the affected card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle Card active to off">
    Switch the **Card active** toggle to the off position and confirm. All new transactions are blocked within seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact Husk support">
    Reach out via the in-app chat or email **support@usehusk.com** to report the card as lost or stolen. The support team will permanently block the card and arrange a replacement.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Freezing a card yourself only pauses it — you must also contact Husk support to formally report a card as lost or stolen so a replacement can be issued and the original card permanently blocked on the Mastercard network.
</Warning>

## Replace a physical card

Replacement cards are issued at no charge for lost or stolen cards. Once support confirms the original card is blocked, a new card is ordered automatically and shipped to your registered address. Standard delivery timelines apply.

If your card is damaged or expired, request a replacement from the card detail page by clicking **Request replacement**.
