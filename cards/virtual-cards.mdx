Virtual cards are Mastercard numbers that exist entirely in software — no plastic, no delivery wait. You get a 16-digit card number, expiry date, and CVV the moment you create one, making them perfect for vendor subscriptions, SaaS tools, team software licences, and any online purchase where you want a dedicated, controllable payment method.

## When to use virtual cards

- **Recurring SaaS subscriptions** — one card per tool means you can spot unused subscriptions immediately and cancel cleanly without touching other payments.
- **Vendor-specific payments** — isolate spend with any single supplier so your finance team sees exactly what each vendor costs.
- **One-time online purchases** — create a card, make the purchase, then delete the card to prevent future charges.
- **Team members without a physical card** — anyone on your team can have a virtual card with their own spending rules without waiting for delivery.

<Tip>
  Create a separate virtual card for every SaaS vendor. When you want to cancel a subscription, freeze or delete that card instead of hunting through a shared card statement. You'll also instantly see if a vendor attempts to charge after cancellation.
</Tip>

## Create a virtual card

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Cards section">
    Log in to [app.usehusk.com](https://app.usehusk.com) and click **Cards** in the left-hand navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Issue card">
    Select **Issue card** in the top-right corner of the Cards page, then choose **Virtual card** from the card-type selector.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the card">
    Give the card a descriptive name — for example, *AWS Production*, *Figma Team*, or *Conference Travel*. This name appears on your transaction feed and makes reconciliation easier.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign to a team member">
    Select the team member who will use the card from the **Assign to** dropdown. The card will appear in their dashboard view and any spend notifications will be routed to them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a spending limit (optional)">
    Toggle on **Spending limit** and enter a daily, monthly, or per-transaction cap. You can also leave this open and apply limits later. See [Spending Controls](/cards/spending-controls) for more detail.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Issue the card">
    Click **Issue card**. The full card number, expiry, and CVV are displayed immediately. Copy the details or share them securely with the assigned team member directly from the dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set card-level spending limits

Every virtual card can have its own independent limit, separate from any other card. To adjust limits on an existing card:

1. Go to **Cards** and click the card you want to update.
2. Select **Edit limits**.
3. Choose from **Per transaction**, **Daily**, or **Monthly** limit types — you can set one, two, or all three simultaneously.
4. Enter the limit amount in your account currency and click **Save**.

Changes take effect immediately on the next transaction attempt.

## Freeze a virtual card

Freezing a card prevents any new transactions while keeping the card number and history intact. To freeze a card:

1. Open the card from the **Cards** list.
2. Toggle the **Card active** switch to the off position.
3. Confirm the freeze in the modal.

The card is frozen instantly. Toggle the switch back on at any time to unfreeze it.

## Delete a virtual card

Deleting a card permanently invalidates the card number. Any future charge attempt on that number will be declined.

1. Open the card from the **Cards** list.
2. Click **Delete card** at the bottom of the card detail panel.
3. Confirm deletion in the modal.

<Warning>
  Deleting a card is irreversible. Make sure no active subscriptions or automated payments depend on the card number before you delete it. If you're unsure, freeze the card first and monitor for declined transaction alerts before proceeding to delete.
</Warning>

## View virtual card transactions

All transactions appear in real time in the **Transactions** tab, filterable by card name, team member, date range, and merchant. Attach receipts via WhatsApp or the dashboard — see [WhatsApp Receipts](/expenses/whatsapp-receipts) for the full workflow.
