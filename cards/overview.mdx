Husk corporate cards are Mastercard debit and charge cards issued by Stripe Technology Europe Limited, authorised by the Central Bank of Ireland (firm reference C187865). Every card is designed for the pace of startup life — you can issue cards in seconds, set granular spending rules per card, and get real-time visibility into where your company's money is going, all from one dashboard.

## Two types of card

Husk offers virtual cards and physical cards. Both run on the Mastercard network and are accepted at over 100 million merchants worldwide.

## Key benefits

- **Instant issuance** — virtual cards are ready to use the moment you create them; no waiting, no forms to post.
- **Per-card controls** — set unique spending limits, allowed merchants, and merchant-category rules on every single card.
- **Cashback** — earn cashback on eligible card spend, credited automatically to your Husk balance.
- **Global acceptance** — pay in 150+ currencies across 210 countries, anywhere Mastercard is accepted.
- **30-day repayment on charge cards** — consolidate spending and repay at the end of the billing cycle.
- **No personal guarantee** — credit decisions are powered by live business data pulled directly from your connected accounts, not your historical balance sheet.

## How credit decisions work

Traditional business cards rely on audited financials and director guarantees. Husk reads your live revenue, cash balance, and burn rate through PSD2 open banking connections and makes a credit decision in real time. This means you can access a charge card even if your company is less than a year old, as long as the underlying business data supports it.

<Info>
  Credit limits are reviewed automatically as your business data changes. Connecting more bank accounts and accounting tools gives Husk a fuller picture and may increase your available limit.
</Info>

## Getting started

To issue your first card, log in to the dashboard at [app.usehusk.com](https://app.usehusk.com) and navigate to **Cards → Issue card**. You can issue a virtual card in under a minute. For physical cards, follow the ordering steps in the [Physical Cards](/cards/physical-cards) guide.
