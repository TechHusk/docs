Husk Financial Insights gives you a single, always-on dashboard that pulls together every transaction, category, and trend across your business. Instead of stitching together spreadsheets or waiting for month-end reports, you see what's happening with your money right now — from how fast you're burning cash to which vendors have quietly raised their prices.

## What Financial Insights Includes

Husk combines your corporate card transactions with live bank data via PSD2 open banking to build a complete picture of your finances. The moment a card is swiped or a bank feed refreshes, your dashboard updates — no manual imports required.

Key capabilities available on the Insights dashboard:

- **Burn Rate** — your net cash outflow averaged over the past three months, updated continuously
- **Cash Flow** — inflows vs. outflows broken down by time period and category
- **Runway** — how many months of runway remain at your current burn rate
- **Spending Patterns** — category-level breakdowns showing where budget is going and how it trends over time
- **Vendor Price Trends** — automatic detection of price changes from recurring vendors so you spot creeping costs before they compound
- **Multi-Bank View** — unified balances and transactions across all connected accounts via PSD2

## How Data Is Sourced

Husk pulls financial data from two complementary sources:

1. **Card transactions** — every purchase made on Husk virtual or physical cards is captured instantly, tagged with merchant data, and categorised automatically.
2. **Open banking (PSD2)** — connect your existing bank accounts (Wise, Revolut Business, traditional banks, and more) with read-only access. Husk aggregates balances and transaction history into the same dashboard without the ability to move funds.

Together, these two feeds give you a complete view of company spending — not just what goes through Husk cards, but everything flowing through your operational accounts.
