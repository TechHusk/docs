Husk offers three plans designed to grow with your company — from solo founders validating an idea to scale-up finance teams managing complex, multi-entity spend. Every plan includes access to the full Husk platform, card issuing controls, vendor and category spend controls, 3D Secure on all cards, and dedicated Slack support. The differences come down to team size, card allowances, and cashback rates.

<Note>
  All prices shown are **exclusive of VAT**. Applicable VAT will be added at checkout based on your country of incorporation and VAT registration status.
</Note>

## Additional fees

The following fees apply on top of your monthly plan and are consistent across all tiers unless noted.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Foreign exchange (FX) fees">
    Transactions in a currency other than EUR are subject to an FX conversion fee:

    | Plan | FX fee |
    |---|---|
    | Free | 2% |
    | Startup | 2% |
    | Growth | 1% |

    The fee is applied to the converted EUR amount at the time of the transaction.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Card payments outside the EEA">
    Payments made with your Husk card at merchants located outside the European Economic Area incur an additional fee of **1% + €0.30 per transaction**, applied on all plans.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Chargebacks">
    If you raise a dispute and a chargeback is processed, a fee of **€25.00 per chargeback** applies, regardless of the outcome. We recommend contacting the merchant directly before initiating a formal dispute to avoid this fee where possible.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Changing your plan

You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from **Settings → Billing** in the Husk dashboard. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

For full details on managing your subscription, adding seats, and understanding your invoice, see [Plans & Billing](/account/plans-billing).
