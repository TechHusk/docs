Husk's WhatsApp receipt capture removes the single biggest friction point in expense management: getting receipts from your team. Instead of asking employees to log into a portal or fill in an expense form, they simply snap a photo of their receipt and send it to the Husk WhatsApp number. Husk handles everything else — reading the receipt, extracting the data, and matching it to the right card transaction.

## How WhatsApp receipt capture works

The flow is deliberately simple:

1. Make a purchase with your Husk card.
2. Take a photo of the receipt (or save the PDF).
3. Send it to the Husk WhatsApp number.
4. Husk AI extracts the data and matches the receipt to your transaction.

That's it. No app to open, no form to fill in, no email to send — just the messaging app your team already uses every day.

## Set up WhatsApp receipt capture

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Husk dashboard">
    Log in to [app.usehusk.com](https://app.usehusk.com) and navigate to **Expenses → Receipt Capture** in the left-hand sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect WhatsApp">
    Click **Connect WhatsApp** and follow the on-screen instructions. You'll be shown the Husk WhatsApp business number and a QR code you can scan to start the chat immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the chat link with your team">
    Copy the shareable Husk WhatsApp link from the **Team Setup** tab and send it to your colleagues via Slack, email, or however your team communicates. Anyone with the link can start submitting receipts straight away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send your first receipt">
    Ask a team member to make a test purchase, snap the receipt, and send it to the Husk WhatsApp number. Within moments the extracted details will appear in the **Reconciliation** queue on the dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Husk extracts automatically

When a receipt arrives via WhatsApp, Husk's AI engine reads it and pulls out the following fields without any manual input:

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| **Merchant name** | Deliveroo, AWS, WeWork |
| **Amount** | €42.50 |
| **Currency** | EUR, GBP, USD, and more |
| **Date** | 14 Jun 2025 |
| **Category** | Meals & Entertainment, Software, Travel |

These fields are pre-filled on the expense record and can be edited at any time from the dashboard if a correction is needed.

## Supported file types

You can send receipts in the following formats:

- **Photos** — JPEG or PNG taken directly on a phone camera
- **Screenshots** — from email confirmations or online checkouts
- **PDF documents** — invoices, e-receipts, and statements

<Tip>
  Before sending a receipt, type a short note in the WhatsApp message — for example *"Team lunch Berlin"* or *"AWS prod account July"*. Husk attaches your note to the expense record, making categorisation and search significantly easier later.
</Tip>

## Processing invoices in 107+ languages

Husk's OCR and extraction engine supports more than 107 languages, so your team can submit receipts from anywhere in the world — in the local language — without any manual re-entry. Whether it's a restaurant bill in Japanese, a hotel folio in Arabic, or a contractor invoice in Polish, Husk reads it accurately and maps it to the correct fields.

<Info>
  Currency conversion is handled automatically. Husk records both the original currency and the EUR equivalent at the time of the transaction, keeping your reporting consistent.
</Info>

## What happens after submission

Once a receipt lands in Husk via WhatsApp:

1. The AI engine extracts all fields within seconds.
2. Husk searches your recent card transactions for a matching amount, merchant, and date.
3. If a match is found, the receipt is paired with the transaction and the expense is marked **Reconciled**.
4. If no match is found yet (for example, the transaction is still pending), the receipt waits in the **Pending Match** queue and is paired automatically once the transaction clears.
5. Reconciled expenses are immediately available in your real-time expense reports and ready to export to your accounting tool.

See [Reconciliation](/expenses/reconciliation) to learn how to review, approve, and export matched expenses.
