## Core features

### WhatsApp receipt capture

Your team doesn't need to log into a dashboard or install another app. They snap a photo of their receipt and send it to the Husk WhatsApp number. Husk's AI engine extracts the merchant name, amount, date, category, and currency — then links the receipt directly to the matching card transaction. See [WhatsApp Receipts](/expenses/whatsapp-receipts) for setup instructions.

### Automatic reconciliation

Every card transaction made on a Husk card enters the reconciliation pipeline immediately. When a matching receipt arrives, Husk pairs them and marks the expense as reconciled. Unmatched transactions surface in a clear review queue so your finance team can resolve exceptions without combing through spreadsheets. See [Reconciliation](/expenses/reconciliation) for the full workflow.

### Real-time expense reporting

Expense reports in Husk are always live. There is no month-end crunch, no manual export, and no waiting for employees to submit their claims. Filter by team, card, merchant, category, or date range at any time and export directly to your accounting tool.

## How expenses fit into the Husk workflow

Husk expense management sits at the centre of your financial operating system, connecting cards, receipts, and accounting in a single loop:

1. **Spend** — a team member pays with their Husk virtual or physical card.
2. **Capture** — they send the receipt via WhatsApp (or upload it directly).
3. **Extract** — Husk AI reads the receipt and pulls out all relevant fields.
4. **Match** — the receipt is automatically paired with the card transaction.
5. **Sync** — reconciled expenses are pushed to Xero, QuickBooks, or NetSuite.
6. **Report** — real-time dashboards reflect every transaction as it happens.
