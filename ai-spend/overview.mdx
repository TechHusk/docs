AI costs have a way of sneaking up on you. One month you're running a few thousand GPT-4 calls for a beta feature; the next you're staring at a $40,000 invoice split across OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google with no clear story for your board. Husk AI Spend Management pulls every provider bill into a single, attributed view so you always know what you're spending, what it's buying, and whether it's worth it.

## The problem with AI costs today

Most teams track AI spend the same way they track a utility bill — they pay it, wince, and move on. The real problem isn't the size of the invoice; it's the opacity. Your costs are scattered across multiple providers, none of which use the same billing model. Attribution is an afterthought: you know you spent €18,000 on LLM calls last month, but you have no idea which feature drove it, which customers consumed the most, or whether switching models would improve or destroy your margins.

That uncertainty gets expensive fast. Without clean AI economics, you're flying blind into fundraising conversations, pricing decisions, and infrastructure planning.

## What Husk AI Spend Management gives you

Husk connects to your AI provider billing APIs and your payment data, then overlays the tagging and attribution layer your engineering team already uses (or can add in minutes). The result is a live, reconciled view of your AI P&L:

- **Cost by provider and model** — see exactly what each provider and each model version costs you in real time, not 30 days after the fact
- **Cost by feature or label** — tag your API calls with a feature name (e.g. `summarisation`, `search`, `chat`) and Husk maps spend to product surface
- **Cost per customer** — attribute every AI call to a customer ID and see whether each account is actually profitable to serve
- **Model switch simulator** — model the margin impact of switching from GPT-4o to GPT-4.1, or from Claude Sonnet to Haiku, before you commit
- **Billing anomaly detection** — Husk flags double charges, quiet price increases, and unexpected usage spikes across every provider
