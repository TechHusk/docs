The Husk Slack integration brings your financial activity into the place your team communicates. Instead of logging into the dashboard to check for missing receipts or over-budget alerts, Husk surfaces the right information at the right time — directly in Slack. You can also query your balances and spend totals on the fly by mentioning the Husk bot in any channel.

## What the Slack integration does

- **Spend alerts** — notifies you when a card transaction is posted, including amount, merchant, and cardholder
- **Budget threshold alerts** — pings your chosen channel when a category or team budget reaches a configured percentage
- **Receipt missing reminders** — reminds cardholders to attach a receipt when one hasn't been uploaded within your configured window
- **Approval notifications** — routes spend approval requests to approvers and confirms when they're approved or declined
- **Balance summaries** — receive a scheduled daily or weekly summary of your account balances and burn rate

## Connect Husk to Slack

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations in the Husk dashboard">
    Log in to [app.usehusk.com](https://app.usehusk.com) and go to **Settings → Integrations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Slack">
    Find the Slack tile and click **Connect**. You'll be redirected to Slack's OAuth authorisation page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Husk to your Slack workspace">
    Click **Allow** on the Slack authorisation screen. Make sure you're authorising the correct workspace — Husk will only be able to post to the workspace you select here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your default notification channel">
    Back in Husk, select the Slack channel where you want spend alerts and notifications to be posted by default. You can use an existing channel or create a new one in Slack first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your settings">
    Click **Save** to activate the integration. Husk will post a confirmation message to your chosen channel to confirm the connection is live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Create a dedicated **#finance-alerts** channel in Slack before connecting Husk. Keeping financial notifications separate from general channels makes it easier for your finance team to monitor alerts without the noise, and gives you a clean audit trail of all spend activity.
</Tip>

## Configure notification types

Once connected, fine-tune exactly what Husk posts and where. Go to **Settings → Integrations → Slack → Notification settings**.

<Accordion title="Transaction alerts">
  Post a message to Slack every time a Husk card transaction is authorised. You can choose to notify:
  - All transactions
  - Transactions above a specified amount (e.g. only transactions over €50)
  - Transactions from specific cardholders or teams

  Assign a dedicated channel for transaction alerts if you want to keep them separate from other notifications.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Budget threshold alerts">
  Get notified when a category or team budget reaches a threshold you define. Common settings:
  - **75% spent** — early warning to review or reallocate budget
  - **90% spent** — urgent alert for approvers
  - **100% spent** — immediate notification that a budget is exhausted

  Each threshold can route to a different channel or tag a specific person.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Receipt missing reminders">
  Husk automatically reminds cardholders in Slack if they haven't attached a receipt within your configured window (default: 48 hours). The reminder goes to the cardholder directly as a Slack DM.

  You can adjust the reminder window and choose whether to send a follow-up reminder if the receipt is still missing after a second period.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Approval notifications">
  When a spend request or out-of-policy transaction requires approval, Husk sends a Slack message to the designated approver with a one-click approve or decline action. The requester receives a notification once a decision is made.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Balance and burn rate summaries">
  Schedule a daily or weekly digest to your chosen channel summarising:
  - Current account balances
  - Spend vs. budget for the period
  - Burn rate and runway estimate

  Useful for keeping leadership informed without requiring them to log into the dashboard.
</Accordion>

## Query Husk from Slack with the bot

Once connected, you can mention **@Husk** in any channel or DM to ask quick financial questions without leaving Slack.

**Example queries:**

```
@Husk What's our current balance?
@Husk How much did we spend on SaaS this month?
@Husk Who has outstanding receipts?
@Husk What's our runway at the current burn rate?
```

The Husk bot responds with a formatted summary pulled live from your dashboard data.

<Note>
  The Husk bot only responds to members of your Husk workspace. Users who are in Slack but not invited to your Husk account will see a message asking them to request access.
</Note>
