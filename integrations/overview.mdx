Husk is designed to slot into the tools your team already uses. Whether you're reconciling transactions in Xero, routing alerts through Slack, or querying spend data from Claude, every integration is built to reduce manual work and keep your financial picture accurate and up to date.

### Accounting

| Tool | Use case |
|---|---|
| **Xero** | Two-way sync of transactions, categories, receipts, and VAT data |
| **QuickBooks** | Automated export of reconciled expenses and category mapping |
| **NetSuite** | Enterprise-grade sync with custom field mapping — contact support to set up |

### HR & Payroll

| Tool | Use case |
|---|---|
| **Gusto** | Sync employee data for card issuance and expense policy assignment |

### Productivity

| Tool | Use case |
|---|---|
| **Slack** | Spend alerts, budget threshold notifications, and receipt reminders |

### Travel & Expense

| Tool | Use case |
|---|---|
| **Navan** | Import travel and accommodation expenses directly into Husk for reconciliation |

### Banking

| Tool | Use case |
|---|---|
| **Wise** | Connect your Wise Business account for multi-bank cash flow visibility |
| **Revolut Business** | Pull Revolut Business balances and transactions into the Husk dashboard |

### AI & MCP Clients

| Tool | Use case |
|---|---|
| **Claude** | Query transactions, balances, and spend trends using natural language |
| **ChatGPT** | Ask financial questions directly from your ChatGPT workspace |
| **Any MCP client** | Connect any Model Context Protocol-compatible client to the Husk MCP server |
