Husk is designed to get you up and running fast. In a single session you can connect your bank accounts, issue cards to your team, and have expense capture flowing through WhatsApp — no lengthy onboarding calls or manual setup required. Follow the steps below to go from zero to a fully operational financial OS.

<Steps>

  <Step title="Create your Husk account (≈ 3 minutes)">
    Head to [app.usehusk.com](https://app.usehusk.com) and click **Sign up**. You'll be asked for:

    - **Your work email** — a verification link will be sent here.
    - **Company details** — registered name, country of incorporation, and business address.
    - **Your role** — so Husk can tailor the dashboard to your responsibilities.

    Check your inbox for the verification email and click the link to confirm your address. Once verified, you'll land directly in your Husk dashboard.

    <Tip>
      Use your company domain email address. This makes it easier to invite teammates later and keeps your account tied to your organisation.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect your bank account via open banking">
    Husk uses **PSD2 open banking** to give you a consolidated, real-time view of all your business bank accounts — including Revolut Business, Wise, and any other PSD2-supported institution.

    1. In the dashboard, navigate to **Accounts → Connect Bank**.
    2. Search for your bank or payment provider.
    3. Authenticate directly with your bank using their secure login flow — Husk never stores your banking credentials.
    4. Grant read access and return to the Husk dashboard.

    Your balances, transactions, and cash flow data will begin populating immediately.

    <Note>
      You can connect multiple bank accounts across different providers. All balances are aggregated into a single view so you always see your true cash position.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Invite your team members">
    Bring your finance team, founders, or department leads into Husk so they can manage their own cards and expenses.

    1. Go to **Settings → Team**.
    2. Click **Invite member** and enter their email address.
    3. Assign a role — **Admin**, **Finance Manager**, or **Employee**.
    4. Click **Send invite**.

    Invited members receive an email with a link to set up their own Husk login. Their cards and expense limits are tied to their individual profile.

    <Tip>
      Your plan determines how many members you can add. Free includes 3 members, Startup includes 5, and Growth includes 10. You can always add extra seats — see [Plans & Pricing](/pricing-plans) for per-seat rates.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Issue your first virtual card (≈ 20 seconds)">
    Virtual cards are the fastest way to start spending. You can create one in seconds and use it immediately for online purchases, SaaS subscriptions, or any card-not-present payment.

    1. Navigate to **Cards → Virtual Cards**.
    2. Click **New virtual card**.
    3. Give the card a name (e.g. "AWS Production" or "Google Ads").
    4. Set a spending limit and, optionally, restrict it to a specific vendor or category.
    5. Click **Create card**.

    Your new card number, expiry, and CVV appear instantly and are ready to use.

    <Note>
      All Husk cards — virtual and physical — are issued by **Stripe Technology Europe Limited**, authorised as an e-money institution, and run on the **Mastercard** network. Your cards are covered by Mastercard's global acceptance and security standards, including 3D Secure authentication.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Capture your first receipt via WhatsApp">
    Never lose a receipt again. Husk's WhatsApp receipt capture lets any cardholder snap a photo of a receipt and submit it in seconds — no app download required.

    1. Save the **Husk WhatsApp number** to your phone (you'll find it in **Settings → Integrations → WhatsApp**).
    2. Make a purchase on your Husk card.
    3. Open WhatsApp and send a photo of the receipt — or just forward the email receipt screenshot — to the Husk number.
    4. Husk's AI reads the merchant, amount, date, and VAT, then matches it to the correct card transaction automatically.

    The reconciled transaction appears in your dashboard within seconds, tagged and ready for export to your accounting software.

    <Tip>
      You can also add a short note in the WhatsApp message (e.g. "Client dinner – Q2 pipeline") and Husk will attach it as a memo to the transaction.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

</Steps>
