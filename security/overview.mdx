Husk is built on a regulated, multi-layered security foundation so you can move fast without compromising control. Every card is issued by Stripe Technology Europe Limited, an authorised e-money institution regulated by the Central Bank of Ireland (firm reference C187865), and runs on the Mastercard network. On top of that, Husk adds 24/7 real-time transaction monitoring, 3D Secure authentication on every card, PSD2-compliant read-only bank connections, and end-to-end data encryption — all included from the moment you sign up.

## Regulated card issuing

All Husk corporate cards are issued by **Stripe Technology Europe Limited**, authorised and regulated by the **Central Bank of Ireland** as an e-money institution (firm reference **C187865**). This means your card programme operates inside a well-established European regulatory framework with mandatory safeguarding requirements for your funds.

## Mastercard network protections

Every Husk card runs on the **Mastercard network**, giving you global acceptance across 210+ countries and built-in network-level fraud detection. Mastercard's Zero Liability policy means you are not held responsible for unauthorised transactions reported in a timely manner.

## 3D Secure on every card

3D Secure (Mastercard Identity Check) is **enabled by default on all Husk cards, on every plan — at no extra cost**. When you or a team member makes an online purchase at a 3DS-enabled merchant, an authentication challenge confirms the identity of the cardholder before the payment is authorised, dramatically reducing the risk of card-not-present fraud.

<Info>
  3D Secure shifts fraud liability away from your company and onto the merchant or issuer in most cases. There is nothing you need to configure — it is on by default.
</Info>

## 24/7 real-time transaction monitoring

Husk's monitoring engine watches every transaction as it happens, flagging unusual activity including large out-of-pattern charges, out-of-policy spend, category violations, and duplicate charges. Alerts surface instantly in your dashboard notification centre and, if you have the Slack integration enabled, directly in your chosen channel.

## PSD2-compliant open banking

Bank account connections for the **Multi-Bank Visibility** feature are established through read-only PSD2 open banking APIs. Husk never stores your online banking credentials — connections are authorised directly with your bank via a secure OAuth-style flow and can be revoked at any time from the dashboard.

## Data encryption

All data transmitted between your browser or mobile device and Husk servers is encrypted in transit using **TLS 1.2+**. Sensitive data at rest — including card details and financial records — is encrypted using **AES-256**. Husk employees are subject to strict access controls and audit logging.
