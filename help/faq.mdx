Got a question about Husk? You are in the right place. Below you will find answers to the questions we hear most often — from whether Husk is a bank to how FX fees are calculated. If you cannot find what you are looking for, reach out via in-app chat or email **hi@usehusk.com** and the team will get back to you.

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="Who can use Husk?">
    Husk is built for **European startups and scale-up teams**. If your business is registered in a supported European country and you have at least one team member managing company spend, Husk is designed for you. Whether you are a pre-seed startup with three people or a scale-up with hundreds of employees across multiple countries, Husk grows with you through its Free, Startup, and Growth plans.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is Husk a bank?">
    No — Husk is not a bank. Husk cards are issued by **Stripe Technology Europe Limited**, an e-money institution authorised and regulated by the **Central Bank of Ireland** (firm reference **C187865**). E-money institutions are regulated financial entities that must safeguard customer funds, but they do not offer credit products or take deposits in the same way that banks do. Your funds are safeguarded in accordance with the EU Electronic Money Directive.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need a credit history to get cards?">
    No credit history is required. Rather than relying on traditional credit scoring, Husk uses **live business data** — such as your current bank balances and recent financial activity — to assess your account. This means newly incorporated companies and startups without a credit track record can still access Husk corporate cards.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is a personal guarantee required?">
    Never. Husk does not ask founders or directors to provide a personal guarantee at any point. Your personal finances are entirely separate from your Husk account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How quickly can I get a card?">
    **Virtual cards** are issued in as little as **20 seconds** after your account is approved — you can add them to Apple Pay or Google Pay immediately and start spending online right away.

    **Physical cards** are produced and dispatched after you request them in the dashboard. Delivery times depend on your country, but you will receive tracking information once your card is in the post.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which countries does Husk support?">
    Husk serves **European businesses** — your company must be registered in a supported European country to open an account. Once you have a card, you can use it for purchases in **210+ countries worldwide** wherever Mastercard is accepted. Check the [Husk website](https://www.usehusk.com) or contact support for the current list of supported registration countries.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are Financial Insights available on the Free plan?">
    No. **Real-time Financial Insights** — including burn rate, cash flow tracking, and runway calculations — are available on the **Startup (€19/month)** and **Growth (€65/month)** plans only. The Free plan gives you access to corporate cards and basic expense management. Upgrade at any time from **Settings → Plans & Billing** in the dashboard.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does WhatsApp receipt capture work?">
    WhatsApp receipt capture lets any team member submit expense receipts without logging into the dashboard. Here is how it works:

    1. A team member makes a purchase and receives a receipt (paper, email, or digital).
    2. They open WhatsApp and send the receipt image or PDF to **Husk's dedicated WhatsApp number** (found in your dashboard under **Expenses → WhatsApp Setup**).
    3. Husk's AI reads the receipt, extracts the merchant name, amount, date, and VAT, and automatically matches it to the corresponding transaction.
    4. If a match is found, the transaction is marked as reconciled. If the receipt cannot be matched automatically, it is flagged for manual review.

    This works for any team member — they do not need a Husk account to submit receipts via WhatsApp.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What accounting tools does Husk integrate with?">
    Husk integrates with the three most widely used accounting platforms among European startups and scale-ups:

    - **Xero** — sync transactions, reconcile receipts, and push expense data automatically.
    - **QuickBooks** — two-way sync for categories, expenses, and VAT codes.
    - **NetSuite** — enterprise-grade integration for scale-ups with more complex finance operations.

    Connect your accounting tool from **Integrations → Accounting** in the dashboard. See the [Accounting Integrations guide](/integrations/accounting) for step-by-step setup instructions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are the FX fees?">
    FX fees depend on your plan:

    | Plan | FX fee on card spend |
    |---|---|
    | Free | 2% |
    | Startup | 2% |
    | Growth | 1% |

    For **payments outside the EEA** (e.g., international transfers via Wise), an additional fee of **1% + €0.30** applies on all plans. FX fees are displayed in real time on the transaction confirmation screen before you approve a payment.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I connect multiple bank accounts?">
    Yes. Husk's **Multi-Bank Visibility** feature lets you connect multiple business bank accounts — including accounts at Revolut Business, Wise, and traditional banks — using **PSD2 open banking** connections. This gives you a consolidated view of your cash position across all accounts in a single dashboard. Connections are read-only; Husk cannot move money from your connected bank accounts. See [Multi-Bank Visibility](/insights/multi-bank) for setup instructions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the MCP integration?">
    The **MCP (Model Context Protocol) server** lets you query your Husk financial data directly from AI assistants like **Claude** or **ChatGPT** — and any other MCP-compatible client. Once connected, you can ask natural-language questions such as "What is our current runway?" or "Which team has the highest software spend this quarter?" and get live answers pulled from your Husk account. Set it up from **Integrations → MCP** in the dashboard or follow the [MCP integration guide](/integrations/mcp).
  </Accordion>
