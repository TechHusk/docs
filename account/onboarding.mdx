Getting started with Husk takes just a few minutes. You'll create your account, verify your company's identity through our automated KYB process, connect your bank account via open banking, and issue your first card — all from a single guided flow in the dashboard.

<Note>
  Husk uses live business signals — such as company registration data, open banking cash flow, and trading history — to make credit decisions. You won't be asked for a personal guarantee.
</Note>

## What you'll need

Before you begin, have the following to hand:

- Your **company registration number** (e.g. from Companies House in the UK, KBO in Belgium, or your local business registry)
- The **registered business address** on file with your national registry
- **Director details** (full legal name, date of birth, home address) for the KYC check
- **VAT number** (if your business is VAT-registered — optional at this stage)
- Access to your **business bank account** for the open banking connection

<Tip>
  Look up your company registration number on your national registry (e.g. [Companies House](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/) in the UK or [KBO/BCE](https://kbopub.economie.fgov.be/) in Belgium) before starting. Having it ready speeds up the KYB step significantly.
</Tip>

## Account setup walkthrough

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [app.usehusk.com](https://app.usehusk.com) and click **Get started**. Enter your work email address and choose a strong password, then click **Create account**.

    You'll land on the onboarding checklist that guides you through each remaining step.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify your email">
    Open the verification email sent to your inbox and click **Confirm email address**. The link is valid for 24 hours.

    If the email hasn't arrived within a couple of minutes, check your spam folder or click **Resend verification email** on the confirmation screen.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your company details">
    Fill in your company's profile:

    - **Legal company name** — exactly as registered with your national business registry
    - **Company registration number** — used to pull live data from your registry
    - **Registered address** — pre-populated once your registration number is confirmed; update if it has changed
    - **VAT number** *(optional)* — enter this if your business is VAT-registered

    Husk cross-references the details you enter with live registry data to confirm they are accurate before proceeding.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect your bank account via open banking">
    Husk uses PSD2-regulated open banking to read your business bank account data. This connection is read-only — Husk cannot move money on your behalf.

    Select your bank from the list of supported providers (Wise, Revolut Business, and major European banks are supported), authenticate via your bank's own app or portal, and approve the data-sharing consent. The connection is established instantly.

    Connecting your bank gives Husk the cash flow signals it needs to assess your business and set your initial card limit.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete identity verification (KYB/KYC)">
    Husk verifies both your **company** (Know Your Business) and the **beneficial owner or authorised director** (Know Your Customer).

    **KYB — what's checked:**
    - Company registration number and status (active, registered address, incorporation date)
    - Ultimate beneficial owners (UBOs) holding 25 %+ of the company
    - Any adverse registry flags

    **KYC — what's checked:**
    - Full legal name and date of birth of the authorising director
    - Government-issued photo ID (passport or national ID card) — you'll be prompted to upload or scan this in-app
    - Liveness check via your device camera

    Most accounts are approved **instantly** when live business data is available. In rare cases where manual review is needed, you'll hear back within one business day.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Issue your first card">
    Once your account is approved, you'll be taken straight to the **Cards** section. Click **Issue card** to create your first virtual card — it's ready to use immediately in Apple Pay, Google Pay, or for online payments.

    To order a physical Mastercard, select **Order physical card**, confirm your delivery address, and expect delivery within 3–5 business days.
  </Step>
</Steps>
