Your Husk subscription, invoices, and payment details are all managed from **Settings → Billing**. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time, and changes take effect according to your current billing cycle.

<Note>
  All prices shown in Husk are exclusive of VAT. If your business is located in an EU member state and Husk is required to charge VAT under applicable rules, the applicable rate will be added to your invoice automatically.
</Note>

## Viewing your current plan

Open the dashboard, click your company name in the top-left corner, and go to **Settings → Billing**. The **Plan** section shows:

- Your active plan (Free, Startup, or Growth)
- Your next billing date
- The number of team members on your account and any per-user add-on charges
- A summary of your current month's charges

For a full comparison of what each plan includes, see the [Pricing plans](/pricing-plans) page.

## Upgrading your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings → Billing">
    In the Billing section, click **Upgrade plan** next to your current plan name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your new plan">
    Review the plan comparison and click **Select** on the plan you want to move to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the upgrade">
    Review the charge summary, which includes a **prorated amount** for the remainder of the current billing period. Confirm by clicking **Upgrade now**.

    Your new plan features are available **immediately** after the upgrade is confirmed. The prorated charge is billed to your card on file straight away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Downgrading your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings → Billing">
    In the Billing section, click **Change plan**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a lower plan">
    Choose the plan you want to move to and click **Downgrade**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the downgrade">
    Review what features you'll lose access to, then click **Confirm downgrade**.

    Your current plan's features remain active **until the end of your current billing period**. At the next renewal date, your account switches to the lower plan and the lower price is charged.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Downgrading to a plan with a lower member limit will restrict the ability to invite new members once you exceed that limit. Existing members above the limit will remain active until you manually remove them, but you will be billed for them as per-user add-ons until the end of the billing period.
</Warning>

## Billing cycle

Husk bills **monthly, in advance**. Your billing date is set to the day you first subscribed to a paid plan. For example, if you upgraded to Startup on 10 March, your next billing date is 10 April.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Upgrade mid-cycle">
    When you upgrade mid-cycle, Husk calculates the prorated cost for the remaining days in the current billing period and charges that amount immediately. From your next billing date, you are charged the full new plan price.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Downgrade mid-cycle">
    When you downgrade, no refund is issued for the current period. Your current plan stays active until your next billing date, at which point the new lower price takes effect.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Additional members">
    Per-user add-on charges are also billed monthly in advance, prorated from the date the member was added. Removing a member stops the charge from the next billing cycle.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Invoices and receipts

All invoices are generated automatically and available in **Settings → Billing → Invoice history**.

To download an invoice:

1. Find the billing period you need in the invoice list.
2. Click the **Download PDF** button to the right of the entry.

Invoices include your company name, registered address, VAT number (if provided), a line-item breakdown of charges, and the applied VAT rate. They are suitable for submission to your accountant or accounting software.

## Payment method

Husk charges your **card on file** at each billing date. To update your payment card:

1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Payment method**.
2. Click **Update card**.
3. Enter your new card details and click **Save**.

The new card is used for all future charges. If a payment fails, Husk will retry the charge after 24 hours and notify the account Admin by email.

## Cancelling your subscription

To cancel, go to **Settings → Billing**, click **Cancel subscription**, and follow the confirmation steps. Your paid plan remains active until the end of the current billing period. After that, your account moves to the **Free** plan — cards remain active but plan-specific features are disabled.
