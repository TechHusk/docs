Husk is built for teams. You can invite colleagues, assign roles that match their responsibilities, issue each person a card with tailored spending limits, and remove access instantly if someone leaves. All team management lives in **Settings → Team**.

<Tip>
  Assign the **Admin** role only to people in your finance or operations team. Admins can view all transactions, adjust card limits, and modify billing settings — keep that access tight.
</Tip>

## Roles

Husk has two roles. Every invited member must be assigned one.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Admin" icon="shield-halved" href="/account/invite-team">
    Full access to the dashboard. Admins can invite and remove team members, issue and freeze cards, set spending controls, view all transactions across the company, manage integrations, and update billing settings.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Member" icon="user" href="/account/invite-team">
    Personal card access only. Members can view and manage their own card, upload receipts via WhatsApp or the dashboard, and view their own transaction history. They cannot see other people's cards or company-wide data.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Member limits by plan

The number of team members (including the account owner) you can add depends on your plan.

| Plan | Included members | Additional members |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | 3 | Not available |
| **Startup** | 5 | +€3 / user / month |
| **Growth** | 10 | +€5 / user / month |

If you need more members than your plan allows, either add them as paid extras (Startup and Growth) or [upgrade your plan](/pricing-plans).

## How to invite a team member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team settings">
    In the Husk dashboard, click your company name in the top-left corner and select **Settings**. Then choose **Team** from the left-hand menu.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    Click **Invite member**. Enter the person's **work email address**, select their **role** (Admin or Member), and click **Send invite**.

    The invited person receives an email from Husk with a link to accept the invitation. The link is valid for 72 hours.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Invited member sets up their account">
    When the invitee clicks the link in their email, they are prompted to:

    1. Set a password for their Husk account
    2. Verify their email address
    3. Complete a brief identity check (name, date of birth, and a photo ID scan) — required before a card can be issued to them

    Once their identity is verified, their account is active and they can log in to [app.usehusk.com](https://app.usehusk.com).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Issue the new member a card">
    After the member's account is active, navigate to **Cards → Issue card**, select the team member from the dropdown, choose **Virtual** or **Physical**, and set any spending limits you want to apply.

    Virtual cards are available immediately. Physical cards are delivered within 3–5 business days.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing existing members

### Changing a member's role

Go to **Settings → Team**, click the three-dot menu next to the member's name, and select **Edit role**. The change takes effect immediately.

### Removing a team member

Go to **Settings → Team**, click the three-dot menu next to the member's name, and select **Remove member**.

<Warning>
  Removing a team member **freezes their card immediately**. Any pending transactions will still be processed, but no new transactions can be made. If the member has an outstanding expense report, make sure it is reconciled before removing them.
</Warning>

Their transaction history remains visible to Admins in the dashboard for audit and accounting purposes.
